When a creator withdraws, USDC is converted into local currency and sent to the creator's mobile money account through an offramp partner.

## Pretium

Pretium handles the conversion from USDC to mobile money. You may see their name in the withdrawal flow or in payout references.

The KES amount depends on the rate shown when you request the withdrawal. The current minimum withdrawal is {minimumWithdrawal}. Withdrawals are {withdrawalTimeline}.
