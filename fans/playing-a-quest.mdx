Some creators attach a quest — a short quiz with a leaderboard — to the content they sell.

## Finding the quest

If a creator has an active quest, you will see a quest card on their payment page showing the quest title, how many questions it has, your attempt limit, and the current leader.

Unlock the content first. The quest opens after your payment confirms.

## Taking the quest

Answer the questions in order. Depending on what the creator set up, you may see multiple choice questions, true or false statements, written answers, or a file to upload.

Your progress is saved as you go, so a lost connection will not wipe your answers.

## Attempts and scoring

Each quest allows between one and five attempts. Multiple choice and true or false answers score immediately, and the creator may include an explanation for each one. Written and uploaded answers are scored once the creator reviews them.

## The leaderboard

Submitted scores appear on the quest leaderboard with your display name and your score out of the maximum. Some creators reward the top scorer or the top three.

## Claiming a badge

If the creator attached a completion badge, you can claim it to your wallet after you submit your attempt. You need a connected wallet to receive it.

<Note>
  Claim the badge with the same wallet you used to pay, so your purchase and
  your badge stay together.
</Note>
