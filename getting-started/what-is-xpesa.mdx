{productName} is the African-native platform for creators and educators to sell and teach what they know: documents, packs, gated links, paid sessions, and tips. Creators can price content in dollars, receive USDC, and withdraw earnings to M-Pesa.

It exists for creators and educators who already sell through DMs, links, and manual payment screenshots, but want a cleaner flow: one checkout, automatic access, quizzes and rewards to keep learners coming back, and a payout path that makes sense locally.
