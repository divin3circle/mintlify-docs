Every quest keeps a score. What you do with those scores is up to you.

## Reward modes

## Completion badges

You can attach an NFT completion badge to a quest. Give the badge a name, a short description, and upload the artwork. Once the quest is live, a buyer who submits their attempt can claim the badge to their wallet.

Badges are minted on Avalanche, the same network XPesa uses for payments.

<Note>
  Upload the badge artwork before enabling the badge. A badge without artwork
  cannot be claimed.
</Note>

## The leaderboard

Each quest has a public leaderboard showing rank, player name, and score out of the maximum. Buyers reach it from the quest card on your payment page.

You can hide the leaderboard while you are still setting a quest up, then turn it on when you are ready to compete.

## Reviewing attempts

Multiple choice and true or false answers score automatically. Open-ended and file answers land in a review queue, where you can score them yourself or use AI-assisted review to speed up a large batch. You can export the results when you need them outside the dashboard.

## Analytics

Quest analytics show how many buyers started, how many submitted, and how scores were distributed. Use it to spot questions that everyone gets wrong — usually a sign the wording or your source material needs work.

<Tip>
  A quest with a badge and a visible leaderboard gives buyers a reason to
  finish your content, not just buy it.
</Tip>
