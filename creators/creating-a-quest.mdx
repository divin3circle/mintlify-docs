A quest is a quiz attached to one of your paid links. Buyers unlock your content first, then answer your questions and compete on a leaderboard.

Use quests when you want buyers to actually work through what they bought — exam prep, course material, onboarding packs, or lesson notes.

## Building a quest

Open **Quests** in your dashboard and choose **Create**. The builder has two steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the quest">
    Pick the paid link the quest belongs to, then add a **Quest name** and
    **Quest description**. Set the reward mode, the attempt limit, and whether
    the leaderboard is visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your questions">
    Write each question, mark the correct answer, and set how many points it is
    worth. Add an explanation if you want buyers to see why an answer was
    right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Question types

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multiple choice" icon="list">
    Offers two to four options with one correct answer. Best for quick recall
    checks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="True or false" icon="check">
    A single statement to accept or reject. Best for fast, unambiguous facts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Open ended" icon="pen">
    Accepts a written answer. These need your review before they are scored.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="File upload" icon="paperclip">
    Accepts a file as the answer. Use it for worked solutions, designs, or
    assignments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Each question carries its own point value, so you can weight harder questions more heavily.

## Generating questions with AI

Instead of writing every question yourself, you can generate a starter set from the quest and its linked content. Generation produces between 3 and 10 multiple choice and true or false questions, each with options and an explanation.

<Tip>
  Treat generated questions as a draft. Read every one and fix anything that
  does not match your material before you publish.
</Tip>

## Attempt limits

Set **Max attempts** between 1 and 5. One attempt keeps the leaderboard competitive; more attempts suit practice material where repetition helps.

## Publishing

A quest starts as a draft. Publish it to make it active, and end it when the challenge is over. Buyers only see active quests on the payment page.

<Note>
  A quest belongs to one paid link. To run the same quiz on another offer,
  create a second quest on that link.
</Note>
